My research focuses primarily on theoretical cosmology. I study how cosmological observations can be used to gain insights about fundamental physics. I am particularly interested in the ways that we can utilize observations of the cosmic microwave background and large scale structure to learn about the fundamental building blocks of the universe, the properties and interactions of dark matter and neutrinos, and the physics of the early universe. {% endcomment %}

I am a theoretical cosmologist working at the interface between theory and data. I develop techniques that maximize the scientific return from cosmological surveys, particularly of the cosmic microwave background, and I work out what the resulting measurements imply for fundamental physics, from the properties of neutrinos and the dark sector to the physics of the early universe and gravitational waves.
