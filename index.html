My research focuses primarily on theoretical cosmology. I study how cosmological observations can be used to gain insights about fundamental physics. I am a theoretical cosmologist working at the interface between theory and data. I develop techniques that maximize the scientific return from cosmological surveys, particularly of the cosmic microwave background, and I work out what the resulting measurements imply for fundamental physics, from the properties of neutrinos and the dark sector to the physics of the early universe and gravitational waves.
